# Monetization

Ideas for earning from the game, talked through with the owner on 2026-10-12. **Nothing here is built, priced or finally decided.** The owner wants to explore it more later. The game is free today.

## Direction (owner, 2026-10-12)

Sell **temporary power-ups** rather than permanent passes. A boost that runs out can be bought again, and it never permanently shortens the 50-hour camp.

- A boost is measured in **play time**: it counts down only while the player is playing.
- The owner's example: **one hour of boosted play, to be used within 24 hours of buying it.**
  - Claude's reading of "a 24 hour window for an hour of playtime boost": the hour counts down only during play, and whatever is left is lost 24 hours after the purchase. To confirm with the owner when this is picked up again. The other reading is that the hour never expires.

## Boosts talked about

| Boost | What it does | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Double Wood | Banked wood counts twice | The main one. Multiplies on top of wood worth, so ten times at the cabin stage instead of five. The owner's own example. |
| Extra stamina | A bigger stamina bar for a while | The owner's own example. Claude suggests keeping it modest, about a fifth more, because the chase must stay tricky; the cabin perk is only a tenth. |
| Material sense | The next material glows from further away | Claude's idea. Saves searching without touching the chase. |
| Save My Haul | Keep the wood you were carrying when the guardian catches you | Claude's idea. A one-off purchase offered at the moment of being caught, not a timed boost. |

## Rules Claude proposed (the owner said "this sounds good" to the whole outline)

- **No boosts in a race.** Everyone is equal there, as with the camp perks. Only looks would ever carry into a race.
- **Nothing that makes the guardian easy.**
- **One of each kind at a time.** Buying a second adds time; it does not double the effect again.
- **The time left always shows on screen.**
- **A free short boost for coming back each day** is worth considering. It lets players try one before buying, and gives the daily reason to return that `CAMP_PROGRESSION.md` lists as missing from Version 0.

## Other ideas raised, not chosen

- **Permanent game passes.** A permanent Double Wood pass could come later, at a high price, for players who keep buying the timed one.
- **Wood bundles.** Buying wood outright. Should stay small next to late build prices so paying shortens the grind and does not remove it.
- **Cosmetics.** Golden axe, coloured torch flame, lumberjack outfits, camp looks (roof colour, a flag). No effect on balance, but each needs art.
- **Material compass.** An arrow to the next material. Makes the search trivial.

## How Roblox pays

- **Developer products** can be bought again and again; timed boosts and Save My Haul would be these. **Game passes** are bought once and kept.
- Roblox keeps roughly 30% of each sale, and Robux can only be cashed out above a minimum balance, so early income is small.
- Roblox also pays a share when Premium members spend time in a game. That needs no work and rewards daily play.

## Things to settle later

- Which boosts to sell first, how long each lasts, and the prices.
- The 24-hour reading above.
- **Leaderboards.** Double Wood puts buyers ahead on the camp progress board. Best haul and the race boards use raw wood and are unaffected. The camp board could rank by raw wood banked instead.
- **Value grows with progress.** An hour of double wood is worth far more at the cabin than at the lean-to, for the same price. Claude would leave it, as it rewards the most invested players.
- Whether boosts appear in a shop on the menu, in the game, or both.
- When to build it. Claude suggested after the cabin is settled and before the homestead, since early players are the most likely to buy; the owner has not decided.

## What building it involves

A small shop screen, handling of purchases (with the time bought saved alongside the player's progress, so it survives leaving and coming back), and a countdown on the display.
