# Camp Progression: Fire Pit to Village

The plan for how the camp grows, agreed with the owner on 2026-10-08. Only the lean-to stage is built. Everything else here is a plan, and no code depends on it yet.

**Wood prices after the lean-to stage are set for a game of about 50 hours** (see the summary). They rest on one rough timing and are to be checked by timing the tent camp once it is built.

## Rules

- Builds are completed in a fixed order, one at a time. The next build shows at camp as a glowing outline.
- A build costs banked wood plus materials found in the forest. Only the next build's materials appear, one at a time, and a player carries one per trip. A material can only be banked with wood in the haul.
- Seven stages. Each stage has more builds than the last, and its builds need more materials.
- Finishing a stage makes wood worth more when it is banked, and unlocks one other perk. The multiplier is applied at banking, so what the player carries, the carry slowdown and the chase do not change.
- Other builds are visual only.
- The chase must stay tricky but not impossible. Every perk has to pass that test. Carrying more wood was left out for this reason.
- What is replaced: the tent replaces the lean-to, the cot replaces the bedroll. From the hut on nothing is removed. The tent stays as storage and the hut as the workshop.
- The clearing grows as the camp does, and the forest grows in proportion with it. Sizes per stage are not yet decided (see "Still open").

## Summary

This is the Version 0 design (owner, 2026-10-08). The whole game is meant to take 50 hours or more, so that players keep coming back. The extra time is put in the late game: builds come quickly in the early stages, when a new player is most likely to quit, and slow down once the player has a cabin and is invested.

Play time is estimated from the owner's own figures: the lean-to stage took 30 minutes to an hour, and a trip banks roughly 20 to 110 wood. That is about 95 wood a minute before the multiplier, taking 45 minutes for the stage. It is one rough measurement, so every time below could be out by half in either direction.

The times include wood being worth more in each stage. They do not include the other perks (better axe, bigger stamina bar, map, lantern), a player getting better at the game, or the bigger trees found on the deeper trips that later materials need. All of those make later stages faster than shown, so read the times from the hut on as upper estimates.

## Still open

- **Prices.** Set for about 52 hours. To be checked by timing the tent camp once it is built; if real play comes in short, the later prices go up.
- **A daily reason to return.** Raised by Claude (for example, the first bank of each day worth double). Not in Version 0; the owner will add updates later.
- **Where materials appear.** Each build completed pushes materials 20 studs farther out, and that reaches the forest edge by about the ninth build. As the game stands, every material from the tent camp on would be at the far edge of the forest. The step needs spreading across all 74 builds, or tying to the forest's growth.
- **Tent camp perk.** Carrying more wood was picked first, then dropped to protect the chase. Nothing has replaced it.
- **Clearing and forest sizes per stage.** Today the clearing is 26 studs in radius and the forest edge is at 280. The clearing fits roughly the first three stages. "In proportion" could mean the forest keeps the same depth beyond the clearing, or keeps the same ratio to it (about eleven times), which would make a very large forest by the village.
- **Walking inside the cabin.** The camera is held close under the trees and will need its own handling indoors.
- **Villagers.** Nothing in the game stands around or walks yet. The settlement is the first stage that needs it.
- **Models.** Which pieces are sculpted and which are built in code, stage by stage, with a quiz before each stage is built.
- **Founding a new camp.** What happens after the bell.
