# Scout Patches

A reward system for solo play, raised by the owner on 2026-10-12: boy scout or girl scout style patches earned for doing missions. **Nothing here is built.** The owner wants to go over the ideas before anything is decided.

## What the owner has decided

- Patches are for **single player mode**.
- A patch is earned for **doing a mission**. The owner's example: clearing the entire clearing of trees in one round.
- **Every camp stage finished earns a patch** (owner, 2026-10-13). With the seven stages in `CAMP_PROGRESSION.md` that is seven patches: lean-to, tent camp, hut, cabin, homestead, settlement, village.

Everything below is Claude's suggestion, to be gone over.

## How it might look

- **A sash**, on the menu or in camp, showing every patch: earned ones in colour, the rest as grey outlines, so a player can see what is left to chase.
- Each patch has one clear mission, and pops up on screen with its picture when earned.
- Patches could also be **Roblox badges**, which show on a player's profile where friends see them. A game can create a few badges free each day; beyond that each costs a small amount of Robux.
- Some patches could give a reward, such as a free short boost (see `MONETIZATION.md`).
- A patch could come in levels, bronze, silver and gold, for the same mission done at three sizes.

## Patch ideas

| Patch | Mission | Whose idea |
|---|---|---|
| One per camp stage (7) | Finish that stage | Owner |
| Clear Cut | Fell every tree near camp in one game | Owner (see the note below) |
| Deep Woods | Fell a tree at the very edge of the forest and make it home | Claude |
| Heavy Load | Bank a haul of 100 wood or more in one trip | Claude |
| Close Shave | Reach camp with the guardian right behind you | Claude |
| Night Owl | Fell 50 trees in one game without being caught | Claude |
| Scavenger | Find 25 materials | Claude |
| Early Bird | Play on 7 different days | Claude |
| Race Day | Win a race | Claude; the owner has said patches are for single player, so this may not belong |

**Note on Clear Cut.** "The entire clearing" needs a definition. The whole forest is 420 trees, or 990 once it has grown for the cabin stage, which nobody could fell in one game with the guardian about. A ring of the trees nearest camp, or a set number such as 100 in one game, would be hard but possible.

## To go over with the owner

- Whether patches give anything besides the patch itself.
- Whether they are also Roblox badges.
- Where the sash is seen: the menu, in camp, both, or worn on the character.
- Whether a player who has already finished stages gets those patches straight away. (Claude would give them: the game already knows how many builds are done.)

- The look of the patches. Each needs a picture; a quiz comes before any art.
- Where this sits in the order of work, next to the last three camp stages and monetization.

## What building it involves

Watching for each mission, saving which patches are earned alongside the player's progress, the sash screen, the pop-up when one is earned, and a picture for every patch.
